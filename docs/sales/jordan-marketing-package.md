## The 10-minute demo

1. **The question** (2 min). Open the demo fleet, ask "Does a new deckhand need a safety orientation
   before we get underway?" Watch it stream. Tap the 46 CFR 140.410 chip: the regulation text opens.
2. **The boat matters** (1 min). Point at the vessel pill: inland towboat, Sub M. Say the same
   question on a passenger ferry gets a different answer.
3. **Their manual** (2 min). Ask "What does our TSMS say about the navigation assessment?" The
   answer cites the company document. Say: upload yours once, it's in every answer.
4. **The audit** (2 min). Open the audit log (who asked what, when, with citations) and the Fleet
   Audit Readiness card (expired medical, TWIC due). This is what the TPO sees.
5. **The close** (1 min). "First boat free for 90 days, no card, ten minutes to set up. I'll send the
   link and the one-pager now." Then send them, while you're still on the call.

## Objections you'll hear

- **"We have a binder / the TSMS covers it."** The binder doesn't answer in thirty seconds on deck at
  0400. RegKnot does, and it cites the page in your binder.
- **"Is this legal advice?"** No. It's a navigation aid that reads the regulation and your manual
  and shows you where it's written. Their OCMI and TPO are still their authorities.
- **"Can it be wrong?"** Every citation is checked against the regulation text before the answer is
  shown, and the answer says when something isn't in the library. A wrong section number never
  reaches the crew. Nothing is perfect; this is far better than memory.
- **"Our data?"** Each company's documents are stored separately and only its own crew can see them.
  Deletion is self-serve. Details at regknots.com/privacy.
- **"Does it work offshore?"** It needs a data connection (phone or boat Wi-Fi). It's a web app;
  nothing to install.
- **"International too?"** Yes: SOLAS, MARPOL, STCW, the IMDG Code and 14 flag states are in the
  library. The towing pitch leads with Subchapter M because that's where the pain is.
- **"Price per boat adds up."** Fleet rates from 5 boats (code FLEET5) and 10 boats (FLEET10); one
  invoice for the whole company on request.
