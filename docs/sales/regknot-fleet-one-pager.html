Does a new deckhand need a safety orientation before we get underway?

Yes. Before the boat first gets underway with them aboard: ten topics, from emergency duties and lifesaving gear to falls overboard, PPE and the tow, logged with the date, topics and signatures. 46 CFR 140.410

What records does a Subchapter M towboat have to keep?

Ten kinds, in the Towing Vessel Record, the official logbook or your TSMS: personnel, orientations, drills, examinations and tests, navigation equipment and assessments, training, oil residue, towing gear inspections, fire systems. 46 CFR 140.915
