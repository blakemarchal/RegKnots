## What you're selling

## Who to sell to

**Subchapter M towing companies with 3 to 30 boats.** Inland and coastal towboats and tugs, all under
the same Coast Guard rules (46 CFR Subchapter M) since 2018, most of them running a safety management
system (TSMS) that a third-party organization (TPO) audits. The buyer is the **owner, the safety manager
or the DPA** (designated person ashore). The pain is real: crews ask "is that actually required?" ten
times a week, the answer is in a 500-page binder, and the TPO audit shows up every year.

Where your network helps most, in order:
1. **Marine insurance brokers and underwriters** (hull, P&I). They have the fleet list and a reason
   to want fewer incidents.
2. **TPOs** (six Coast Guard-approved auditors). They see every operator's records. We'd rather
   partner than sell to them.
3. **Maritime lawyers and surveyors** who already advise operators on compliance.
4. **Towing associations:** AWO and the regional groups; port and waterway associations.
5. **Maritime training schools.** Different offer: our free exam practice page for their students
   (regknots.com/practice). Good for goodwill and introductions.

We have a list of **991 towing companies** (from Coast Guard vessel records) with contacts where we
found them, and a phone script. Ask Blake for the list; use it after the warm intros, not instead of them.

## The pitch, in one breath

"Your crew gets a cited answer in thirty seconds instead of a guess. Your TSMS lives in the same
chat. And the audit log shows the TPO that your people are actually using it. First boat free for
90 days, no card, set up in ten minutes."

Three things that land with safety managers:
- **Every citation is checked against the regulation text** before the answer is shown. A wrong
  section number never reaches them.
- **It knows the boat.** Inland river towboat vs. coastal tug vs. a passenger ferry get different
  answers; it reads the vessel profile.
- **Their own manual, too.** Upload the TSMS; questions get answered from it, with page references.

Two real examples (each checked against the regulation):
- *Does a new deckhand need a safety orientation before we get underway?* Yes, ten topics, logged
  with names and signatures, before the boat first gets underway (46 CFR 140.410).
- *What records does a Subchapter M towboat have to keep?* Ten kinds, in the Towing Vessel Record,
  the logbook or the TSMS (46 CFR 140.915).

**Never answer a regulatory question yourself on a call.** Offer to send the cited answer, or put the
question into RegKnot on the spot and read it out. That is the demo.
