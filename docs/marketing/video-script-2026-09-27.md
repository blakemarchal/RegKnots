# RegKnots video script, v1 (2026-09-27)

One shoot gives three pieces:
- **Main, 45 s.** For LinkedIn, Facebook and the website.
- **Two 15 s cuts.** For Reels, Shorts and TikTok.

Shoot vertical (9:16) and crop a 16:9 version for LinkedIn and YouTube. Burn the captions in,
because most people watch muted.

**Rules**
- The answer on screen is a real, unedited RegKnots answer, from a screen recording.
- Karynn's voice is real, from a phone voice memo. If she won't record, use a neutral AI narrator.
  Never clone her voice or build an avatar of her.
- Every regulatory statement below is checked against the corpus text: 46 CFR 140.410 and
  140.915, the same answers as the outreach hooks in `scripts/outreach/hooks.md`.

## What we need from Karynn (5 minutes)

- Record the main voiceover in her phone's voice memo app.
  - A quiet room works best; a closet full of clothes is ideal.
  - Phone 6–8 inches away. Read slowly, three takes, send the file.
- Optional: a 5-second phone clip, in uniform or on the bridge, saying "I'm Captain Karynn Marchal"
  for the 0:30 beat.

## Screen recording (10 minutes: Blake, or Claude in the browser pane)

- Use a demo account with a vessel profile: M/V Demo, towing vessel, Subchapter M, inland/coastal.
- Record at normal speed. Ask the scripted questions exactly as written, then tap the citation chip.
- Use the answers as they come. If one isn't clean, ask again or use another question from
  `scripts/outreach/hooks.md`. Never edit an answer.

## Edit (free)

- **Order:** voiceover, B-roll, screen recording, captions, music.
- **Music:** royalty-free (YouTube Audio Library), well under the voice.
- **Tools:** CapCut (free, auto-captions), or Claude assembles it with ffmpeg from the files.
- **Export:** 1080×1920 vertical and 1920×1080 landscape.
